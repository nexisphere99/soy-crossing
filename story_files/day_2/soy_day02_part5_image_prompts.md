## LOCATION IMAGES

### IMG-D2-001: Transfer Room
**Prompt:** A clinical room the size of a large bedroom with soft overhead lighting. A reclined dental-style chair with a matte black headset device descending from a mechanical ceiling arm. Four prongs extend from the headset at precise angles. Beside the chair, a second bed where a woman lies with eyes closed, breathing slowly, wearing a grey cotton training outfit. Her skin is light brown, hair dark and wavy. The room feels quiet, controlled, neither dramatic nor sterile. Medical but intimate. No chrome, no sci-fi glow. Just soft light and two bodies and a machine connecting them.
**Style:** Painterly digital, warm clinical lighting, intimate scale, muted palette
**Use:** Background for transfer room passages

**Prompt:** Close-up of a neural transfer headset in matte black. Four contact prongs with soft rubber-metal pads positioned to touch the temples, crown, and base of skull. The background is out of focus, clinical room tones.

### IMG-D2-003: Evening Walk, Amber Streetlights
**Prompt:** An urban sidewalk at dusk in October. Streetlights casting amber pools on the concrete. Fallen leaves. A man walking alone, seen from behind, grey t-shirt, hands in pockets. The buildings on either side are storefronts closing for the night. In the distance, a woman walks ahead of him, dark coat, quick stride, bag on her shoulder. The mood is reflective, liminal, between one self and another.
