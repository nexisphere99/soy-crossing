## STYLE GUIDE (Consistent Across All Days)

**Art Direction:** Cinematic realism with warm noir undertones. Think Wong Kar-wai meets A24. Rich shadows, amber/orange color grading, shallow depth of field. Characters should feel lived-in, not polished. Clothing wrinkled, hair imperfect, expressions caught mid-thought rather than posed.

**Color Palette:** Deep warm blacks (#0C0A09), amber accent (#D97706), clinical blue-white for facility scenes, orange sodium streetlight for night exteriors.

**Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 for scene establishing shots, 3:4 for character portraits, 1:1 for phone UI/icons.
