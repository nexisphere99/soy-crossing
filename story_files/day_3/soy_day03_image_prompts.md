### IMAGE 1: Morning Apartment (t0_day03_morning_apartment)
```
Scene: Interior of a small, lived-in New York apartment in pre-dawn October light. A young Latino man (25, brown skin, dark curly hair that needs cutting, shadow under his eyes) stands at a kitchen counter eating dry toast, wearing a black henley with a slightly stretched collar and dark jeans. A grey tabby cat sits on the counter beside where a butter dish used to be, one paw resting on the edge with casual menace. The kitchen is clean but sparse, single plate in the drying rack, single mug, the solitary geometry of a man living alone. A yellow sticky note is folded on the counter. Coffee machine steam rises. Warm amber kitchen light clashes against the cold blue of the pre-dawn window. Through the window: fire escapes, brick, the silhouette of a water tower.

Style: Cinematic photography. Shallow depth of field focused on the man's hands holding toast. Warm interior vs. cold exterior color contrast. Intimate, slightly melancholic. Film grain. Aspect ratio 16:9.
```

### IMAGE 5: Scenario 1 -- The Mirror (t0_day03_scenario1_mirror)
```
Scene: A hotel-like room with warm carpeting, a nightstand, a brass lamp casting amber light. A full-length mirror mounted on the far wall. In the mirror's reflection: a young woman's figure. Auburn hair catching lamplight with copper tones. Pale skin. Grey training clothes. The silhouette shows defined waist, the curve of hips, the shape of breasts under stretched fabric. But the figure is turned partially away from the mirror, caught in the act of turning, the body moving one direction while the eyes linger for one more second. The expression on the reflected face is conflicted: confusion wearing someone else's features, a man's bewilderment mapped onto a woman's bone structure. The tension between looking and looking away. The room feels private, charged, a space where something is being discovered and resisted simultaneously.

Style: Narrative photography. Warm amber and shadow. The mirror is the brightest element, the figure slightly darker. Emphasis on the reflection rather than the physical body. Psychological tension. Painterly quality. Aspect ratio 3:4 (portrait).
```

### IMAGE 6: Scenario 2 -- The Changing Room (t0_day03_scenario2_shower)
```
Scene: A small, warm room with steam curling from a running shower in the corner (glass door, fogged). On a wooden bench: folded grey training clothes. On a wall hook: a white towel. In the center of the frame: a pair of hands (feminine, pale, slender fingers) gripping the hem of a grey training top, fabric bunched at the ribcage, caught in the act of being pulled upward. The frame cuts at the shoulders, we see only hands, fabric, the edge of a white bra band, the tension in the knuckles. Steam softens the background. The lighting is warm and diffuse. The image captures the specific vulnerability of undressing in a body that isn't yours: the intimacy of fabric against borrowed skin, the tremor visible in the grip.

Style: Close-up photography. Warm, steamy atmosphere. Shallow depth of field focused on the hands. Soft focus on background shower steam. Intimate without being sexual. The tension is in the grip, not the skin. Aspect ratio 4:5 (near-square portrait).
```
